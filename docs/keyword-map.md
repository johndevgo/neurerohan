# Search architecture and keyword map

Last reviewed: 2026-08-26

This map converts the supplied keyword exports into one canonical URL per distinct intent. Search volume and difficulty are directional research inputs, not ranking forecasts. Closely related wording belongs on one useful page unless the audience, offer, location, or task is materially different.

## Optimization standard

- One primary search intent and one non-competing keyword cluster per indexed URL.
- Natural primary-term prominence in the title, H1, early answer, relevant subheadings, descriptive links, and image text where appropriate.
- Complete coverage of the decision: definition, fit, process, scope, evidence, limitations, objections, next action, and related entities.
- Short paragraphs, direct answers, useful lists, tables, checklists, and contextual internal links where they improve comprehension.
- Founder insight and verifiable first-party proof instead of invented numbers, reviews, clients, or outcomes.
- Page-specific metadata, canonical URL, indexability, breadcrumbs, and truthful structured data matching visible content.
- No fixed keyword-density target. Google classifies unnatural repetition as keyword stuffing; density is reviewed only as a diagnostic for accidental omission or repetition.

## Cannibalization rules

- Homepage owns the brand; the national agency page owns broad Nepal marketing-company language.
- SEO expert owns direct specialist intent; SEO company owns managed, coordinated implementation.
- Advertising agency owns multi-platform paid acquisition; Google Ads owns the platform-specific Google/PPC decision.
- Social agency owns organic content operations and connected distribution; packages owns scope and price-comparison intent.
- Local SEO owns Maps/Business Profile optimization; Kathmandu owns local agency evaluation.
- The Business Profile guide answers informational queries and links to local SEO; it must not imitate the service page.
- Hotel marketing covers the full hotel growth system; travel SEO remains organic and travel-agency specific.
- Every Insight links to one primary commercial destination plus genuinely related resources.

## Deferred pending evidence or demand

- Meta Ads agency: require a distinct keyword cluster, clear service boundary, and ideally anonymized Meta Ads evidence.
- CRO services, landing-page design, analytics/conversion tracking, content-marketing agency, and technical SEO audit pages: require stronger supplied demand and distinct deliverables before separation.
- Bhaktapur agency page: the location is real, but a dedicated page should wait for local query evidence and additional unique local information.
- Pricing pages for SEO, Google Ads, or digital marketing: require real ranges or examples from Rohan.
- Case-study pages: require permission, role, intervention, baseline, dates, outcomes, and limitations. Dashboard captures alone remain performance evidence.

## Rejected from the supplied export

Do not create money or traffic pages for app/software development, IT-company lists, graphic design, hosting, domains, courses, training, careers, colleges, competitor names, Facebook-ban news, translations, Web 1.0, ride sharing, blockchain, or unrelated company lists. These do not match the current GrowthLabs offer, evidence, or audience.

Do not publish “best,” “top,” or “#1” self-award pages, swapped-city doorway pages, fake review markup, or pages whose only difference is wording.

## Internal linking model

The homepage and `/services` introduce the hierarchy. The national agency page links to platform and specialist services. Service pages link to relevant industry pages and Insights. Insights link back to one primary commercial next step and related resources using descriptive, natural anchors.

After launch, evaluate impressions, query ownership, clicks, qualified conversions, and unintended URL overlap in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools before splitting more pages.
