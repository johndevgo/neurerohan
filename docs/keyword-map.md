## Cannibalisation rules

- The homepage targets the brand, not the national agency phrase.
- Nepal agency owns broad national terms; Kathmandu owns local evaluation.
- SEO expert owns personal-specialist intent; SEO company owns agency/team/ongoing-service intent.
- Social agency owns service intent; packages owns cost, inclusion, and comparison intent.
- Hotel marketing covers the full acquisition system; travel SEO stays organic-search led.
- Do not create location or industry variants by merely swapping nouns.

## Phase 2 editorial backlog

Priority support articles: Facebook lead-generation campaign examples and setup; the GrowthLabs digital marketing process; a modern Google Business Profile/local SEO guide; social media content calendar and template; SEO versus social media; why web design matters; business benefits of SEO; SEO-friendly content; website launch checklist; mobile-friendly website guide; and hotel content planning.

Secondary topics include guest posting as an authority tactic, LinkedIn marketing in Nepal, ecommerce marketing lessons, martech and automation, visual storytelling, TikTok influencer planning, responsible AI-assisted content, and Instagram Reels strategy. Publish only with original expertise, a clear reader need, and a relevant commercial route.

## Deferred or rejected

- Do not target competitor names such as Softbenz, Verisk, Digital Terai, or Jobs Dynamics with dedicated pages.
- Do not build IT company, software company, app development, graphic design, hosting, domain registration, or training money pages unless GrowthLabs genuinely launches those offers.
- Broad traffic topics—business ideas, glossary translations, Facebook-ban news, colleges, careers, company lists, Web 1.0, ride sharing, blockchain, and AI-company lists—are excluded because lead relevance is weak or freshness risk is high.
- No “best/top” claim page should be published without a transparent methodology and verifiable evidence.

## Internal linking model

The homepage and services hub lead to the national agency and core capabilities. The national page links to specialist pages. Industry pages link to the capabilities they use. Phase 2 articles should link contextually to one primary money page and related support articles. Anchors should describe destinations naturally rather than repeat exact keywords mechanically.
